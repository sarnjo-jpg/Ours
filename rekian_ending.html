(01) / i love you

“you make being around you feel easy.”

i love the quiet kind of happiness i get from you. nothing has to be extraordinary. sometimes just talking to you, hearing from you, or knowing you're there is already enough to make my day feel a little better.

(02) / i adore you

“you make ordinary things feel worth remembering.”

there are little things about you that you probably consider ordinary, but i remember them anyway. the smallest moments have a way of staying with me, simply because they happened with you.

(03) / you calm me

“somehow, you make the noise in my head quieter.”

there is something about being with you that feels peaceful. i don't always need an answer or a solution. sometimes i just need your presence, and somehow things feel a little less heavy.

(04) / i cherish you

“i notice more about you than i probably let on.”

i remember the way you speak, the little habits you have, the things you like, and the moments when you don't even realize you're being adorable. i keep those things quietly, because they matter to me.
